Known Limitations
⚠
Read before relying on these figures.
A 0.5% BTC move within 24h occurred on 93–100% of all bars in the tested months, so the win rate is driven chiefly by the window rather than the squeeze filter — at the original 4h window July wins 51.7%. Wins are booked on the 0.5% move without checking whether the 0.05% stop was hit first on the path; run_double_entry() resolves both legs and yields lower results.
